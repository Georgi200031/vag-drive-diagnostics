VAG Drive Diagnostics

Privacy Policy

How the app handles vehicle data, device access, and advertising.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, address, phone number, or contacts.

Vehicle diagnostic data

The app uses a compatible OBD-II adapter to read information such as diagnostic trouble codes, live sensor values, readiness monitors, and vehicle voltage. It uses this information to display diagnostic results and provide diagnostic features. Core diagnostics do not require this information to be uploaded to an external server.

Bluetooth and Wi-Fi

The app uses Bluetooth permissions to discover or access paired devices and communicate with the adapter you select. It can also use the local Wi-Fi network to exchange commands and diagnostic information with a compatible adapter.

Local storage and sharing

Fault history and preferences may be stored on your device. The developer does not sell personal data or intentionally transmit vehicle diagnostic data to third parties. If you use the sharing feature, diagnostic information is sent to the app or service you choose.

Advertising

Versions of the app with advertising enabled use Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses, device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information about the SDK for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This SDK diagnostic information is separate from your vehicle's OBD-II readings.

Where required, the app requests consent before requesting ads. If Google makes privacy options available, you can reopen them in Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children's privacy

The app is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Disclaimer
