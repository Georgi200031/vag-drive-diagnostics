Privacy Policy for VAG Drive Diagnostics

Last updated: September 3, 2026

VAG Drive Diagnostics is an automotive diagnostic application designed to communicate with compatible OBD-II adapters and retrieve diagnostic information from a vehicle.

Information We Collect

VAG Drive Diagnostics does not require users to create an account. The application does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or contacts.

Vehicle Diagnostic Data

The application may read vehicle diagnostic information through a compatible OBD-II adapter. This may include diagnostic trouble codes, live sensor values, readiness monitor information, vehicle voltage, and other information made available through the OBD-II interface.

This information is processed for the purpose of displaying vehicle diagnostic information and providing diagnostic features to the user.

Bluetooth

VAG Drive Diagnostics uses Bluetooth permissions to discover or access paired Bluetooth devices and communicate with compatible Bluetooth OBD-II adapters.

Bluetooth access is used only for establishing communication with a device selected by the user.

Wi-Fi

The application may communicate with compatible Wi-Fi OBD-II adapters over the local network. This connection is used to exchange diagnostic commands and vehicle data with the adapter.

Data Storage

Certain diagnostic information, such as fault history or application preferences, may be stored locally on the user's device to provide application functionality.

Data Sharing

VAG Drive Diagnostics does not sell personal data. The application does not intentionally transmit vehicle diagnostic data to the developer or to third parties.

If the user chooses to use the application's sharing functionality, diagnostic information may be shared through an application or service explicitly selected by the user.

Internet Access

Network access may be used to communicate with Wi-Fi OBD-II adapters on the local network. The core diagnostic functionality does not require diagnostic data to be uploaded to an external server.

Children's Privacy

VAG Drive Diagnostics is not specifically designed for children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Disclaimer

VAG Drive Diagnostics is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Volkswagen AG, Audi AG, or their subsidiaries.

Vehicle and diagnostic information provided by the application is for informational purposes. Users should consult a qualified automotive technician when necessary.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
